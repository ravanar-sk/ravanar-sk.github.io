1. About this policy

Scratch Page is a scratch-and-guess picture game developed by Saravana Kumar for children and families. This policy covers the app on iPhone, iPad, and compatible Apple Silicon Macs, as well as our support and privacy pages.

The app does not require an account. It does not ask for your name, date of birth, phone number, or precise location, and it does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, or contacts. We do not operate a server that receives your saved game progress.

2. What is saved on your device

The app stores information needed to remember your game:

  • Completed levels and your available points balance.
  • Levels unlocked by watching an ad or spending points.
  • Your sound and haptic feedback preferences.
  • The last home-screen picture, so the next surprise can be different.

This information is stored in the app’s local storage. Scratch Page does not provide an account-based cloud sync service. Copies may be included in device backups or transfers managed by Apple, depending on your settings.

Scratching plays bundled sounds and may trigger device vibration. These features do not record audio, pictures, or video.

3. Optional rewarded ads

The first three levels in each category are free. Later levels can be unlocked with 100 in-game points or a Google AdMob rewarded ad. The app also uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy information.

When a connection may start: opening an eligible locked level can begin loading an ad and contact Google before you tap the watch button. You can unlock with points without completing an ad, and points unlocks work offline.

Google’s advertising services may process an IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device or app identifiers where available, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This information can support ad delivery, measurement, service reliability, and fraud prevention. The data processed depends on permissions, device settings, and the advertising configuration. See Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Scratch Page configures ad requests for child-directed treatment and general-audience ad content, requests non-personalized ads, disables publisher first-party identifiers, and tags privacy requests for users under the age of consent. The app does not request Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. Advertising services can still process technical and advertising information under these settings.

Google’s processing, retention, service providers, and international transfers are explained in the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. We do not control Google’s retention of information it processes.

4. Children and families

We design the game for children and apply child-directed advertising settings throughout the app. Scratch Page has no public profiles, chat, or user-uploaded content. Children are not asked to provide personal information to play.

Parents and guardians should handle support messages and privacy questions. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review it and address your request, including deletion of information we hold where appropriate.

5. Your choices and deletion

  • Reset game data: in the app, open Settings → Start a fresh trail and confirm. This clears discoveries, points, and saved level unlocks. Sound and haptic preferences are kept.
  • Manage advertising privacy: Settings → Ad privacy options is shown when Google requires that option. Available choices depend on the privacy flow and your location.
  • Play without completing ads: replay unlocked levels to earn points and use 100 points to unlock an eligible level.
  • Remove remaining local app data: use your device’s app-data controls or delete the app. Backups are managed separately through your Apple settings.

Resetting or removing local app data does not delete information already processed by Google or support email you have sent. Use Google’s privacy resources for its services, or contact us about support correspondence and other privacy requests.

6. If you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to include. We use this information to answer questions, investigate problems, and follow up on your request. It is handled through our email provider and may be retained for those purposes and applicable obligations.

Please send support requests as a parent or guardian and avoid including a child’s name, photos, school, location, or other unnecessary personal information. We do not use support messages for advertising. You can email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of correspondence we hold.

7. These web pages and external links

These support and privacy pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, contact forms, or cookies set by the page code. Their images and styling are served with the pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and access logs, when you visit.

Email links open your email application. Links to Google or other external services are subject to those services’ privacy practices.

8. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version. Please check this page when reviewing a new app release.
