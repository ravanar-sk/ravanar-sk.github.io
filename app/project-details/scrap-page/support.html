Common questions

Tap a question to open it.

Why can’t I select an answer yet?

Answer choices become available once you uncover at least 10% of a level picture. Keep moving across the covered area; rubbing the same spot does not keep increasing the percentage. A single tap usually reveals too little.

Covering the picture again or starting a replay resets the percentage. A wrong choice is disabled for the rest of that round so you can try another answer.

How do I unlock more levels?

Complete levels in order within each category. Levels 1–3 are free. From level 4 onward, choose either Use 100 points or the rewarded ad option.

A points unlock deducts 100 from your available balance. Watching an ad unlocks the selected level only after the ad provider confirms the reward. Each unlock stays saved on that device, and replaying that level does not require another unlock.

Can I earn points by playing the same level again?

Yes. Every correct round earns 10 points, including replays. Choose Play again after a correct answer, or revisit an unlocked level from its category.

A replay starts with a fresh cover and cleared answers. The Points page shows the balance available to spend. The discovery count records each level once, even when you solve it many times. Points are earned in the game; there is no option to buy them with money in the current app.

An ad won’t load, or I closed it without a reward.

New ads need an internet connection and may not always be available. Try loading the ad again, return later, or use 100 points instead. You can replay unlocked levels to earn more points.

Closing an ad before its reward is earned leaves the level locked. Loading failures and presentation errors also leave access unchanged. If you completed an ad but the level stayed locked, email us with the category, level, and approximate time. Please avoid including a child’s personal information.

How do I change the home picture or scratch cover?

Double-tap the home scratch area for another surprise picture and a fresh cover. A fresh home picture is also selected when the app is opened or returns from the background.

On a level, use the cover-again button beside the scratch percentage to start scratching that picture again. Untouched areas stay covered until you scratch them or use the reveal option.

Can I play without using the scratch gesture?

Yes. On a level, open the ? help button and choose Reveal picture without scratching. This fully reveals the picture and enables the answer choices.

For the home picture, use Settings → Reveal the home picture. VoiceOver also provides a Reveal picture action on scratch cards. The app supports larger text and respects your Reduce Motion preference.

How do I control sounds and vibration?

Open Settings → Sound effects to control button sounds, answer chimes, and pencil sounds. Also check device volume, silent mode, and the selected audio output if sounds are missing.

Settings → Haptic feedback controls scratch and answer vibration independently of sound. On supported hardware, Try haptic feedback plays a sample. Make sure the app toggle and your device’s vibration settings are enabled. The app shows an availability message when haptic hardware is unavailable. Vibration cannot be felt in a simulator.

Where are my points and unlocks saved?

Discoveries, points, level unlocks, and preferences are saved locally on your device. No account is needed, and the app does not provide automatic account-based syncing between devices.

Apple-managed backups or device transfers may include app data, depending on your settings. We cannot restore local game data from a developer account. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting if you want to keep your progress.

How do I reset the game or manage privacy?

Use Settings → Start a fresh trail and confirm to clear points, discoveries, and saved level unlocks. This starts your progress over. Sound and haptic preferences are kept.

Ad privacy options appears in Settings when required by Google’s privacy flow. See the privacy policy for local data controls, advertising information, and privacy requests.

Which devices can run Scratch Page?

Scratch Page is designed for iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, and for compatible Apple Silicon Macs running the iPad version. On Mac, use a mouse or trackpad to scratch.

The game uses a portrait layout. Check its App Store listing for the compatibility and availability of your installed version.
